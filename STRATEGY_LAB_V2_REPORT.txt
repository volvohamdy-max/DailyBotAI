STRATEGY LAB V2 — XAUUSD ONLY

- Dynamic condition builder for gold only.
- Timeframes: 5m, 15m, 1h.
- Direction: BUY or SELL.
- Conditions are evaluated on candle close and combined with AND.
- Entry is next candle open.
- Indicators/conditions:
  * Close above/below EMA 9/20/50/100/200
  * EMA crossover up/down
  * RSI(14) above/below custom value
  * ADX(14) above custom value
  * Close above/below VWAP
  * MACD signal crossover up/down
  * Bullish/bearish candle
  * Close above previous high / below previous low
- SL and TP configurable as ATR multiples.
- Backtest report: trades, wins, losses, win rate, Net R, max drawdown, streaks.
- Live bot trading strategy is not modified.
